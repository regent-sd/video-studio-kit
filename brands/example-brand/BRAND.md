# Example Brand: brand guide

This is the made-up brand that ships with the kit. Replace it with your own (say "set up my brand" to Claude, or copy this folder). Claude reads this file and `brand.json` before every edit for this brand.

## Who it is

One paragraph: what the brand sells or makes, who it's for, and what makes it different. Example: *A small neighbourhood bakery that bakes everything by hand each morning. For locals who want something better than the supermarket, and visitors looking for the place the locals go.*

## How it looks

- **Colours:** see `brand.json`. Warm cream backgrounds, dark brown text, a terracotta accent. Never the accent as a full background.
- **Type:** a serif for titles and big numbers, a clean sans for captions.
- **Footage:** bright and airy, slightly warm. Hands at work, close-ups of the product, a finished-product payoff.

## What's on screen

- **Keep out:** anything in `brand.json` → `keep_out`, plus other brands' packaging and logos, screens with customer details, price boards that may change.
- **People:** anyone clearly visible has said yes. No children. Staff only if they agreed.

## Videos for this brand

- Length: 20–40 s for reels.
- Styles that fit: Hands-On Classic for making things, Fast Cut (Brand skin) for explaining.
- Effects: keep them restrained.
- Endings: an honest reaction or a question that asks for comments.
