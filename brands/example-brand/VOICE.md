# Example Brand: voice

How this brand sounds in captions, titles, hooks and anything Claude writes. Claude flags everything it writes for you to approve, and writes it in this voice.

## In three words

Warm, plain, a little playful.

## Do

- Short sentences. Say it the way you'd say it across the counter.
- Real details: the actual number, the actual ingredient, the actual time it took.
- One idea per caption.

## Don't

- No hype words: "amazing", "game-changer", "insane".
- No made-up numbers or claims.
- No emojis in titles (one at the end of a caption is fine).

## Words we use / words we avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| fresh this morning | artisanal |
| made by hand | handcrafted perfection |

## Sample lines

- Hook: "Six hours for one croissant. Here's why."
- End: "Which one would you pick?"
