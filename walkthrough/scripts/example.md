For: new users of Mossy (the kit's made-up demo app, web). Recorded on the demo app with
made-up plants. Target about 0:40.
Take: `rig/takes/example.mjs`. Film: `films/example.film.json`.

## 1. Intro
[screen] The plant list.
- Here's how to add a plant to Mossy, and keep track of when it needs water.

## 2. Add a plant
[screen] Add plant → Name: Peace lily → Room: Bedroom → Water every 7 days → Save.
- Click Add plant.
- Type its name, pick the room, and set how often it needs water.
- Then click Save. It shows up at the top of your list.

## 3. Mark it watered
[screen] Basil card → Mark watered → the badge changes.
- When you water a plant, click Mark watered.
- Mossy starts counting again, so you always know what's next.

## 4. Find a room
[screen] Room filter → Kitchen.
- To see just one room, pick it up here.

## 5. Outro
[screen] Back to All rooms.
- That's it. Every plant in one place, and none of them forgotten.
