# What to say to Claude

The phrases that work. Swap the part in angle brackets for your own words. You don't have to say them exactly: Claude understands the same request in other words, these are just the ones it's sure of. The Studio page's **What to say** tab shows this list; click a phrase to copy it.

## Get started

- `set up my studio`: installs the tools (asking before each download), writes your settings, sets up the house rules, a brand, a sound library, your voice clone and the demo. `continue the setup` picks it up again after a restart.
- `set up my brand` / `add a brand for <name>`: Claude interviews you about a brand's colours, fonts, logo, voice and what must never be on screen, and shows a preview card.
- `change my brand colours` / `change my brand voice`: the same interview, for one part.
- `show me the house rules`
- `add a house rule: <rule>` / `drop the house rule about <topic>`

## Open things

- `open the studio`: opens the Studio page, the front door.
- `open video review`: opens Video Review, where you watch a cut and leave notes.
- `open the footage log`: opens the Footage and Videos tabs.

## Make an edit

- `start a new edit`: opens a new project card (or fill one in on the Studio page).
- `start the card <name>`: Claude reads the card, your rules, your brand, the storytelling guide, the style and the glossary, then shows you a plan, with any effects and the moment each one lands on. Nothing gets built until you say go.
- `make this a Fast Cut` / `do <shoot> as Talking Head Classic` / `Hands-On Classic, please`: names the style. If you don't name one, Claude asks.
- `apply my notes on <video>`: Claude fixes only your open notes and exports a new version (v2, v3…).
- `approve this one`: Claude finishes the package (Finished folder, cover, caption, tracker, a copy in Downloads), names anything new it made and adds what your notes taught it to your storytelling guide.
- `call it <name>`: when Claude asks you to name something new it made.
- `use that again`: lets a look or effect made for one video be reused (it comes off the in-review list).
- `what needs me?`: Claude lists what the Studio home shows: edits in progress, open notes, videos in review and ready to post.

## Footage

- `log new footage`: Claude logs every new clip, moment by moment.
- `find footage of <something>`: Claude searches the Footage Log.

## Posting

- `what's posted`
- `what's still pending`
- `mark <video> posted`
- `mark <video> scheduled` / `shelve <video>`

## Effects

- `/effects`: shows every effect and when each one fits.
- `/spotlight <video> at "<word>"`: puts one effect on a video. Every effect has its own command; they're listed on the What to say tab and in the Glossary.
- `put a <kind of> effect on the line about <something>`: Claude picks the effect and shows you where it would go.
- `show the effects line for <video>`: Claude saves the video's effects map, so Video Review shows a row naming the effect or layout on screen over each stretch. New videos get one when they're exported; this is for any that don't.

## Sound

- `show the sound tracks for <video>`: Claude saves the video's sound map, so Video Review shows its voice, music, footage sound and each sound effect on separate rows. New videos get one when they're exported; this is for any that don't.
- `no effects`: the video without sound effects.
- `quieter effects` / `louder effects`
- `build me a starter sound library`: Claude finds free CC0 sounds, listens to them with Earshot and shows you the list before downloading.

## Voice

- `write a voiceover for <video>`: Claude drafts the script for you to approve, then makes it in your own voice (VoiceStudio) only after you OK the words.

## Walkthroughs and the demo

- `make a walkthrough video of <app or site>`: a narrated product walkthrough (`walkthrough/`).
- `set up the demo shoot`: downloads 14 free stock clips (about 163 MB, it asks first) into a demo shoot and gives the talking clip a narration track (`demo/README.md`).
- `make the demo samples` (or `run the demo`): renders a sample of every style and effect from the demo shoot, so you can see what each looks like.

## Photos, logos and brand files (the Asset Library)

- `open the asset library`: the search page for everything in your asset folders, with pictures.
- `find a photo of <something>` / `find the <logo, menu, label>`: Claude searches the Asset Library and gives you the links.
- `make a post about <something>`: Claude picks pictures from the Asset Library, skipping old logos and anyone who hasn't given an OK, and shows you the picks first.
- `log new assets`: after you add files to your asset folders, Claude lists, previews and tags the new ones.

## Teach the studio

- `add this to the classroom: <link>`: a reel you like for its editing. Claude breaks it down into a classroom card.
- `add this to ideas: <link>`: a reel you like for what it says, such as a strategy or a way to shoot. Claude writes an idea card, kept apart from the editing references.
- `add to the guide: <rule>`: Claude adds the rule to your storytelling guide, with today's date.
