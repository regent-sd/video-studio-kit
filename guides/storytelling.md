# How you like a story told

Claude reads this before planning any edit. It grows from your own notes: when a note you leave on a video says how you like things in general ("cut my hesitation at the start", "music only over the payoff"), Claude adds it here under the right heading, in your words, with the video and the date (`guides/after-approval.md`, step 4). A one-off fix doesn't go here.

It starts with a few **starter lessons** that hold for most short videos. Each is marked *(starter)*. Keep, change or delete any of them; once you've written your own rules, the starter ones are just a default.

Where your preference isn't known yet, it's a question at the bottom, not a rule. Claude asks instead of guessing.

## The hook

- Open on the strongest moment. Showing the payoff first works. *(starter)*
- Hook in the first second. *(starter)*
- Start on the action, not the pause before it. Cut any hesitation at the start of a take. *(starter)*
- The first frame has to read at a glance: level, clear, nothing confusing. *(starter)*

## Pacing and cuts

- Tight and filler-free. *(starter)*
- Never cut a word short; check cuts against the sound. *(starter)*
- Keep the line that bridges two parts of the story. *(starter)*
- Show steps in the order they really happen. *(starter)*

## Voice and words

- Your own words first; anything Claude writes is flagged before you post. *(starter)*
- Only real numbers on screen. *(starter)*

## Captions and on-screen text

- A caption shows once; never across a cut. *(starter)*
- A tag stays up long enough to read and matches what's on screen. *(starter)*
- Moving footage, not still frames. *(starter)*
- Captions name exactly what's there (the real ingredient, the real part, the real setting). *(starter)*

## B-roll, people and the frame

- Clean frames: no clutter, nothing from the keep-out list. *(starter)*
- Product shots close up. *(starter)*
- Faces clearly lit. *(starter)*
- When someone asks to stay out of a video, keep their voice if it's needed and play it over other footage. *(starter)*

## Sound and music

- Every video gets its own music. *(starter)*
- Music arrives with energy. *(starter)*
- Sound matches the picture. *(starter)*
- No stray bangs, knocks or bits of words. *(starter)*

## Endings

- End on an honest reaction, a clear result, or a question that asks for comments. *(starter)*
- Nothing after the last line that matters. *(starter)*

## By brand

One short paragraph per brand once you know: which styles fit, how long its reels run, how restrained the effects are, how its videos end. The details (colours, fonts, keep-out list, voice) live in `brands/<brand>/`.

## Questions for you

1. How long should your reels run? (Most short videos land between 20 and 45 seconds.)
2. Should every video end with an ask (comment, follow, visit), or only some?
3. Music under talking videos, or only under footage without speech?
4. Do you want a few hook versions of each video to test?
5. Family, kids, customers and staff in videos: fine by default, or ask each time?
